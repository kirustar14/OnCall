The problem

Spoken information has no memory.

A trauma bay produces more facts per minute than any one person can hold, and almost none of them get written down while they still matter. The failure is not skill.

Said once

A detail, at 2am

The mother mentions a severe penicillin allergy. Twelve seconds later somebody orders a penicillin-class antibiotic. Both facts were in the room. Neither was in the chart.

AllergyIntolerance · never written

“Someone find out whether she’s anticoagulated.” No name attached, no system holding it, and no one notices when the answer never comes back.

What it does

Details. Tasks. Context.

Three things, and deliberately nothing else. Voice in, voice out — nobody stops to type, and there is no screen anyone has to look at.

Details

Every fact said out loud

Becomes a structured FHIR resource carrying the exact quote and who said it, so the basis is inspectable rather than trusted.

Observation · AllergyIntolerance · MedicationRequest

Tasks

Every request said out loud

Becomes a tracked task with an owner. If nobody was named it stays unowned, and the agent chases it out loud on a timer.

Task · Task.owner · Provenance

Context

Anyone walking in cold

Asks once and gets a spoken catch-up in twenty seconds: what is known, what is pending, and what is still unowned.

spoken handoff · five panels, not a transcript

The line it does not cross

It never recommends a therapy.

What it says

“Ampicillin-sulbactam is a penicillin-class antibacterial. Penicillin allergy documented at 14:32, severe, relayed by the nurse from the mother.”

states the fact · names the source · stops

What it will never say

“Give vancomycin instead.”

no alternative drug · no dose · no “give this”

The boundary is deliberate. Software that recommends a therapy is a different regulatory and liability object than software that surfaces information a clinician can independently review, and the second is the honest description of what a language model is reliably good at. The clinician draws the conclusion. It is also why every assertion carries a Provenance resource — and why tests/test_ledger.py asserts the boundary, so it cannot quietly drift back.

The flow — click any stage

One path, room to room.

Vision A point-of-view camera frame informs context, and never writes a clinical value. A number misread off a monitor at arm’s length would otherwise be indistinguishable from something a clinician actually said.
Watchdog Runs on a timer, independent of anyone speaking, so unowned work gets chased even when the room goes quiet.
Gate Nothing interrupts until the ordered drug’s FDA class is resolved and actually matches a documented allergy.

Why it is allowed to interrupt

The word “penicillin” never appears.

The doctor orders ampicillin-sulbactam. The patient has a documented penicillin allergy. Here is what each approach does with that.

Speaks. Once.
Tier 1 · deterministic

Crosses documented allergies against the medication just ordered, resolves the drug through NIH RxNav to its FDA Established Pharmacologic Class, and fires only on a real class match. It cannot invent a conflict, because it is not the thing deciding one exists.

negative control: clindamycin → Lincosamide → correctly silent

Tier 2 · advisory

An open-ended agent for what no rule could enumerate ahead of time. Read-only: it never writes to the ledger, and it can never promote itself to critical. Two tiers, and only one of them is allowed to raise its voice.

dispatched off the critical path · never blocks tier 1

Grounded in Medplum

The ledger is not a JSON blob.

Task is not a stretch here, it is what the resource is for. Which means the hard part — unowned work — is a standard search rather than a concept we invented.

What the room producesWhere it lands
the requestTask.code.text
why it mattersTask.description
who owns it (omitted entirely when unowned)Task.owner → Practitioner
every unowned, still-open taskGET /Task?owner:missing=true&status=ready
“repeat that pressure in five minutes”Task.restriction.period.end
evidence it was actually doneTask.note · [speech] / [vision]
who asserted it, on whose authorityProvenance.agent.onBehalfOf

Status mapping follows Medplum’s own guidance for a single-system implementation: open → ready, acknowledged → in-progress, completed → completed. Provenance records the agent Device as agent.who and the human source as agent.onBehalfOf, so the mother stays the source and the nurse stays the relay.

The stack, as capability

Each one does something the product could not do without it.

Deepgram

The entire interface, not a transcription step. nova-3-medical streaming in with keyterm prompting, diarization and UtteranceEnd endpointing; Aura-2 speaking back out. There is no keyboard in this product.

STT + TTS · acoustic segments merged into clinical utterances before extraction

Medplum

The record. Every artifact is a real FHIR resource written over OAuth2 client-credentials, which is what makes owner:missing=true a native query instead of a bespoke flag we would have had to maintain.

Task · Provenance · AllergyIntolerance · Observation · MedicationRequest · Practitioner · Encounter

Claude Opus 5

Structured extraction that is stateful: the open ledger goes in with every utterance, so “I’ve got it” resolves against the task it refers to rather than becoming a new one.

structured outputs · schemas pre-compiled at boot · prompt caching on the system block

NIH RxNav

The FDA Established Pharmacologic Class, which is the difference between an interrupt that is a fact and an interrupt that is a guess.

free, no key · cached in process · degrades to silent rather than to wrong
